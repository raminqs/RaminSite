About

Engineering meets machine learning at the edge of infrastructure.

I work at the intersection of civil engineering and artificial intelligence, building data-driven systems that monitor the health of large-scale infrastructure — from highway bridges to railway lines and offshore wind turbines.

My PhD focused on probability-based intelligent structural health monitoring using metaheuristic optimisation, surrogate modelling and big-data techniques. Today I deploy intelligent SHM pipelines that move from raw vibration signals to actionable structural diagnosis.
